Updated Monday, August 3 - 4:35 p.m.
Tuesday's Forecast (August 4)

An approaching trough and associated cold front will shift winds to southwesterly by Tuesday morning. High temperatures will reach the mid 80s across eastern Wisconsin, while counties farther west will remain a bit cooler as the cold front moves through earlier in the day. Ahead of the front, conditions will become favorable for ozone production for counties to the southeast and along the lakeshore. Moderate (Yellow) AQI is forecast for that area, but USG (Orange) AQI will be possible for counties impacted by a sustained lake breeze. Good (Green) AQI for ozone is forecast elsewhere. For PM2.5, some model guidance suggests a decent amount of wildfire smoke in the column behind the cold front from the burning across the western U.S. Although much of this smoke is expected to remain aloft, the precipitation along the front may act to mix some of this smoke down to the surface. As such, a combination of Good to Moderate AQI is forecast for PM2.5.


Wednesday's Forecast (August 5)

Precipitation is expected to persist along the Wisconsin/Illinois border as the cold front continues to slowly move to the southeast, with westerly flow anticipated for most of the state. Ozone should fall mostly in the Good AQI range, with the highest potential for Moderate AQI confined to a small area downwind of the Twin Cities. A mix of Good and Moderate AQI for PM2.5 is forecast to continue, with the highest chances for Moderate AQI near the frontal boundary across southern Wisconsin.

Thursday’s Forecast Trend (August 6)

Air quality is expected to remain sufficient.
