Updated Thursday, July 30 - 4:25 p.m.
Friday's Forecast (July 31)

A low pressure system will move through the region Friday afternoon, bringing widespread showers with a few thunderstorms to Wisconsin. Ahead of the system, southerly winds may prime the southern portion of Lake Michigan with elevated ozone. However, precipitation is expected to begin in the late morning to early afternoon hours, limiting the amount of time that conditions will favor ozone production. Good to Moderate AQI is forecast for both ozone and PM2.5. Moderate AQI potential for ozone will be primarily confined to southeastern Wisconsin ahead of the low pressure system, while chances for Moderate AQI due to PM2.5 will continue statewide as lingering smoke recirculates through the region. Despite the chances for elevated ozone and PM2.5, Good AQI will be the most likely overall.


Saturday's Forecast (August 1)

As the low pressure system continues off to the southeast, flow will become northerly over Wisconsin. High temperatures will be on the cooler side, only reaching the mid to upper 70s. Ozone is expected to be in the Good AQI range. PM2.5 is forecast to be mostly in the Good AQI range, with potential for low Moderate AQI due to recirculating smoke.

Sunday’s Forecast Trend (August 2)

Air quality is expected to remain favorable.
