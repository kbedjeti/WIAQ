Updated Friday, August 28 - 4:45 p.m.
Saturday's Forecast (August 29)

Recirculation of residual wildfire smoke is expected on Saturday as synoptic flow becomes southerly and wind speeds increase. A round of showers is expected to move west to east across Wisconsin throughout the day, bringing cloud cover and limiting high temperatures to the mid 70s. Good (Green) to Moderate (Yellow) AQI for PM2.5 is forecast, with Moderate AQI most likely for central and southern Wisconsin. Ozone is forecast to remain in the Good AQI range.


Sunday's Forecast (August 30)

Flow will remain southerly behind the exiting precipitation on Sunday, pushing high temperatures into the 80s and allowing the recirculation of lingering wildfire smoke to continue. PM2.5 is forecast to remain in the Good to Moderate AQI range, with statewide potential for Moderate AQI. Hotter temperatures and stronger southerly flow will encourage increased ozone production over Lake Michigan. However, trailing precipitation in the morning, cloud cover, and dewpoints in the 60s should also help mitigate ozone production. Moderate AQI for ozone is forecast along the lakeshore, with Good AQI anticipated elsewhere.

Monday’s Forecast Trend (August 31)

Air quality is expected to remain satisfactory.
