Updated Wednesday, August 5 - 5:50 p.m.
Thursday's Forecast (August 6)

Weakening high pressure will move over the Great Lakes region and mostly sunny skies should prevail. Despite dry air over much of the north and west, higher moisture content is expected across eastern and southern Wisconsin, where smoke continues to linger. Therefore, we’ll maintain the Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 forecast in these areas, with Good (Green) AQI forecast elsewhere.


Friday's Forecast (August 7)

Southwesterly flow ahead of an approaching cold front may bring moisture and light smoke further north leading to a larger area of Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5. We’ll also need to keep an eye on ozone near Lake Michigan. For now, we’ll forecast Moderate (Yellow) AQI ozone for all lake shore counties, but Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (Orange) AQI remains possible from Sheboygan County south to Kenosha County.

Saturday’s Forecast Trend (August 8)

Air quality is expected to improve.
