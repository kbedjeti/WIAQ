Updated Sunday, August 30 - 5:10 p.m.
Monday's Forecast (August 31)

A persistent upper level ridge and associated surface high pressure over the southeastern U.S. will lead to multiple days of southwesterly flow and hot temperatures over Wisconsin, with chances for isolated showers and thunderstorms daily. PM2.5 is forecast to be in the Moderate (Yellow) AQI range statewide. Good (Green) AQI is forecast for ozone, with chances for Moderate AQI along the lakeshore if a lake breeze circulation is able to develop.


Tuesday's Forecast (September 1)

Southwesterly flow will continue, with high temperatures reaching the upper 80s to low 90s. Statewide Moderate AQI for PM2.5 is expected to continue. The hotter temperatures will encourage ozone production over Lake Michigan, especially if cloud cover is minimal. However, increased dewpoints aided by precipitation should help mitigate ozone production as well. Moderate AQI for ozone is forecast along the lakeshore, though Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (Orange) AQI cannot be ruled out if a lake breeze front can push onshore.

Wednesday’s Forecast Trend (September 2)

Air quality is expected to remain satisfactory for most, with additional impacts possible along the lakeshore.
