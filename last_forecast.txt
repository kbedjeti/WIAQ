Updated Thursday, July 23 - 5:23 p.m.
Friday's Forecast (July 24)

SSW flow and warmer temperatures are expected alongside mostly sunny skies. Ozone concentrations will potentially reach the Moderate (Yellow) AQI category across eastern Wisconsin, especially within the lake breeze. PM2.5 concentrations will tick up closer to the Moderate (Yellow) AQI category in response to light surface smoke, especially in west-central and northern Wisconsin.


Saturday's Forecast (July 25)

SW-to-W flow will bring another bump in temperatures as well as a slight bump in humidity. Increased cloud cover may help to limit ozone production, but we’ll continue a Moderate (Yellow) AQI forecast for areas within the lake breeze and include a swath downwind of the Twin Cities. Surface smoke from fires in Washington, Oregon, Idaho and British Columbia will also work its way in from the west, potentially leading to statewide Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5.

Sunday’s Forecast Trend (July 26)

Air quality is expected to remain satisfactory, although slightly degraded by wildfire smoke.
