Updated Sunday, August 2 - 10:20 p.m.
Monday's Forecast (August 3)

High pressure will be the primary meteorological driver, with gentle southerly flow, mostly clear skies, and high temperatures in the low to mid 80s. Dewpoints in the upper 50s to low 60s will help keep ozone mostly in the Good (Green) AQI category, with pockets of Moderate (Yellow) AQI possible. A mix of Good and Moderate AQI is also anticipated for PM2.5 due to minor smoke present within the high pressure air mass.


Tuesday's Forecast (August 4)

An approaching trough and associated cold front will shift winds to southerly Monday night into Tuesday. High temperatures will again reach the mid 80s, while northwestern Wisconsin will remain a bit cooler as the cold front moves through earlier in the day. Some precipitation along the front will be possible. Ahead of the front, conditions will become favorable for ozone production for counties to the southeast and along the lakeshore. Moderate AQI is forecast for that area for now, but USG (Orange) AQI is not out of the question for counties impacted by a lake breeze. Good AQI is forecast elsewhere.

Wednesday’s Forecast Trend (August 5)

Air quality is expected to improve.
