Wednesday's Forecast (July 29)

High pressure over Wisconsin will lead to light winds, high temperatures in the mid to upper 80s, and plentiful sunshine. A mix of Good (Green) and Moderate (Yellow) AQI is anticipated, with PM2.5 associated with lingering wildfire smoke likely being the AQI driver.


Thursday's Forecast (July 30)

As high pressure continues to drift south into Illinois and Indiana, winds over Wisconsin will become more southerly. High temperatures will again reach the mid to upper 80s, with dewpoints holding in the 60s. Chances for Moderate AQI for ozone will increase along the lakeshore, particularly within any lake breeze circulation that can develop. Potential for Moderate AQI for PM2.5 will remain possible statewide as diluted wildfire smoke continues to linger over the region.

Friday’s Forecast Trend (July 31)

Air quality is expected to improve for most but may degrade along the southern lakeshore.
