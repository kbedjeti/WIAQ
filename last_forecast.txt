Updated Tuesday, August 25 - 5:00 p.m.
Wednesday's Forecast (August 26)

As a low pressure system passes just north of the Midwest region tomorrow, an associated cold front will move west-to-east through Wisconsin late tonight into Wednesday morning. A few isolated storms along the front will be possible, with a pivot to modest northwesterly flow behind the front by the afternoon hours. Mostly Good (Green) AQI is forecast for PM2.5, with a small chance to see Low-Moderate (Yellow) AQI to the southeast ahead of the boundary. Ozone is forecast to remain in the Good AQI range.


Thursday's Forecast (August 27)

As northwesterly flow continues, a plume of diluted smoke from northwestern Canada is expected to move into Wisconsin. PM2.5 concentrations are forecast to increase into the Moderate AQI range statewide, however, Good AQI may still be observed in some locations. Ozone is forecast to remain in the Good AQI range.

Friday’s Forecast Trend (August 28)

Air quality is expected to remain adequate, with minor improvements possible.
