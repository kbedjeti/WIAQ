Updated Thursday, September 3 - 4:45 p.m.
Friday's Forecast (September 4)

Hot and humid conditions will continue with potentially strong to severe storms moving through during the early a.m. hours. We’ll continue the statewide Good (Green) AQI forecast for now, but chances for Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 because of high dew points or Moderate (Yellow) AQI ozone in south central to southeast Wisconsin because of southerly flow remain possible.


Saturday's Forecast (September 5)

A Canadian high pressure air mass will move in from the northeast, and although temperatures and dew points will moderate some, both parameters will remain above average. Good (Green) AQI should continue for most with the best chance to reach Moderate (Yellow) AQI across western areas.

Sunday’s Forecast Trend (September 6)

Air quality is expected to remain satisfactory.
