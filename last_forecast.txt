Updated Monday, August 31 - 4:00 p.m.
Tuesday's Forecast (September 1)

Southwesterly to westerly flow will continue, with seasonably hot temperatures in the upper 80s to low 90s. PM2.5 is expected to be in the Moderate (Yellow) AQI range statewide. Ozone is forecast to be in the Good (Green) to Moderate AQI range for most of the state, as the heat will encourage production while the elevated dewpoints will simultaneously mitigate production. Model guidance suggests the development of a pool of high ozone over southern Lake Michigan, which may be observed along the Wisconsin lakeshore if a lake breeze front can penetrate inland against large scale westerly flow. Moderate AQI for ozone is forecast along the lakeshore, though Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (Orange) AQI cannot be ruled out.


Wednesday's Forecast (September 2)

Similar conditions are anticipated for Wednesday, with southwesterly flow and seasonably hot temperatures in the upper 80s to low 90s. However, guidance suggests the development of a weak east-west oriented boundary stretching across central Wisconsin, with cooler temperatures north of the boundary. If observed, this boundary will help restrict the highest PM2.5 and ozone concentrations to the southern half of the state. Good to Moderate AQI for both PM2.5 and ozone is forecast, with cleaner air expected farther north. Potential for Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups AQI along the lakeshore will persist, though more confined to the southern and central portions of the lakeshore.

Thursday’s Forecast Trend (September 3)

Air quality is expected to remain adequate for most, with additional impacts possible along the lakeshore.
