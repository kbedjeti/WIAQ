High pressure will drift across the southern Great Lakes region, with winds gaining a southerly component from west to east. We’ll continue the Good (Green) AQI forecast, but there is a chance that vertical mixing could tap into some smoke aloft, leading to Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 potential across northern Wisconsin.

Although mostly Good (Green) AQI PM2.5 is once again expected, smoke aloft may mix down to the surface in northern Wisconsin where Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 will be possible.
