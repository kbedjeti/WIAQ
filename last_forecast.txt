Updated Tuesday, September 1 - 4:15 p.m.
Wednesday's Forecast (September 2)

Seasonably hot and humid conditions will continue across the southern 2/3 of the state. Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 will be forecast within the humid air mass, with Green (Good) AQI expected to the north. Given the hot temperatures and southerly component to the flow, Moderate (Yellow) AQI ozone will be possible along the central and southern Lake Michigan shoreline. Although not expected given the high dew point temperatures, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (Orange) AQI ozone cannot be ruled out for these areas.


Thursday's Forecast (September 3)

Precipitation looks likely for most as the humid air mass surges north. Although any areas of heavy precipitation will help cleanse the atmosphere locally, Moderate (Yellow) AQI PM2.5 will remain possible statewide. Ozone concentrations should be held in check by high dew point temperatures and precipitation/cloud cover, but Moderate (Yellow) AQI ozone remains possible for the southern Lake Michigan shoreline.

Friday’s Forecast Trend (September 4)

Air quality is expected to remain satisfactory.
