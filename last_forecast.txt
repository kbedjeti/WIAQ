Updated Wednesday, August 26 - 3:35 p.m.
Thursday's Forecast (August 27)

A plume of diluted smoke from northwestern Canada is expected to move into Wisconsin throughout the day as northwesterly flow continues behind an exiting low pressure system. PM2.5 concentrations are forecast to increase into the Moderate (Yellow) AQI range for much of the state, with the highest concentrations expected for locations farther west. Anticipating a clean lake breeze, counties along the lakeshore may remain in Good (Green) AQI for PM2.5 instead of seeing smoke impacts. Ozone is forecast to remain in the Good AQI range as well.


Friday's Forecast (August 28)

High pressure will move into the region on Friday, decreasing wind speeds across Wisconsin. Any smoke present at the surface will likely persist, with gentle recirculation expected across the region. Good to Moderate AQI for PM2.5 is forecast, with the southern and western portions of Wisconsin most likely to see Moderate AQI. Good AQI for ozone is forecast to continue.

Saturday’s Forecast Trend (August 29)

Air quality is expected to degrade along the lakeshore.
